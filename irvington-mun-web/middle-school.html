For Middle Schoolers

Middle School MUN

An introduction to Model United Nations, open to middle school students of every age and every level of experience.

The Programme

Start early, start from scratch.

Model United Nations puts you in the seat of a country’s diplomat. You are assigned a nation, almost never your own, and you argue its real positions in committee: researching what that government actually says, speaking in formal debate, building alliances with delegations that want what you want, and writing resolutions that can survive a vote.

Middle School MUN is where that begins. Sessions are run by Irvington’s own delegates and officers, and they assume you know nothing about the UN yet — because most people who walk in don’t. You will learn how a committee runs, how to speak without notes in front of a room, and how to hold an argument together while somebody is trying to take it apart.

There is no tryout and no experience requirement. Come to a meeting, sit in, and see whether it is for you.

Who it’s for

Middle school students of all ages. No experience with Model UN, debate or public speaking is needed to join.

When it meets

Where it’s held

Norse Hall, at Irvington High School in Fremont. Introductory meetings are in room 223.
